# Surface brief: the Africa Twin strip-down page

**Scope:** the whole single-page site (`index.html`, one scrolling surface). **Visitor mode:** Experience; the bike leads from the first viewport and the interface recedes.

**Audience and job:** the main audience is clients judging Mihael's craft, with XRV750 riders as the audience inside the story. The visitor's job is to turn the crank and understand how an RD07 is built, assembly by assembly.

**Proof and content:** verified specs from `research/xrv750-rd07.md`, one card per assembly. The imagery is AI-generated cut paper and is disclosed as such. The site is unofficial, with no Honda logo.

**Constraints:**
- Nothing that reads like an official Honda ad.
- A reduced-motion equivalent (stills plus text).
- Every explanation lives in text, never only in an image.

**Approved comps:**
- `.impeccable/mocks/comp-2b.jpg` is the opening viewport. It is Comp 2 with the gears replaced by a plain crank at the user's request.
- `.impeccable/mocks/comp-3.jpg` is the approved step reference: the lifted part on paper pins, a paper-strip caption, a 01/10 counter and a station line.

**Strip-down method (user decision, 2026-09-27):** paper layers, not video. There are 11 aligned keyframes, from assembled down to one part fewer each time. The difference between neighbouring keyframes is a cut-paper part layer that the crank lifts away.

**Assembly order (the approved rail):** 01 Fairing, 02 Tank & seat, 03 Exhaust, 04 Wheels, 05 Brakes, 06 Front fork, 07 Pro-Link, 08 Cooling, 09 Engine, 10 Frame.

**Unresolved:** whether the keyframe edits stay pixel-aligned enough to cut clean part layers. The fallback is to regenerate an individual step.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The strip-down as a hand-cranked paper automaton. Scrolling turns the crank, and the RD07 opens one assembly at a time in workshop order. It refuses the category default (a dark studio page with glowing callouts) and official-ad gloss.

OWN-WORLD: A kraft-board ground. Pulp-white paper panels with tab-and-slot marks and soft cast shadows. Charcoal ink. One crimson reserved for the assembly in motion: the active rail tab, the crank knob, and the lifted part's fold. Stencil letterpress caps for headings and a condensed workhorse sans for labels. Every control is a paper tab; the active one is crimson and pulled out.

STORY: The visitor meets the whole bike, turns the crank, watches ten assemblies lift off in real order, and reads one verified card per assembly. They end at the bare frame understanding how the bike is built. The unofficial status and the AI imagery are stated plainly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: As in comp-2b:
- **Left:** a headline card, about a fifth of the width, carrying the stencil title, the subline and the unofficial note.
- **Centre:** the paper RD07, large, on a kraft plinth. Three spec tabs sit under the plinth, above a small crank box with a crimson knob.
- **Right:** a vertical "HOW IT COMES APART" rail of ten numbered tabs, with 01 in crimson.
- **Primary action:** the crank, driven by scrolling.

FORM: Crank Automaton, a dealt challenger (catalog: kinetic-sculpture-automata-crank-paper-menagerie), chosen over the assigned #7 Dakar Route Map. Seed key 31bd28af. Signature interaction: scroll turns the crank knob; the active part lifts on paper pins with a crimson fold, its rail tab pulls out, and its caption strip pins in beside it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
